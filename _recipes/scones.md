Ingredients

* * *

|     |       |                              |
| --- | ----- | ---------------------------- |
| 2   | cups  | flour                        |
| ½   | cup   | sugar                        |
| ½   | tsp   | salt                         |
| 2½  |       | baking powder                |
| 1   | stick | butter (frozen)              |
| ½   | cup   | heavy cream (or half & half) |
| 1   |       | egg                          |
| 2½  | tsp   | vanilla                      |
| ½   | cup   | chocolate chips              |

Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. Whisk flour, sugar, salt and baking powder together in a bowl.
2. Grate frozen butter into bowl and combine until the mixture comes together in pea-sized crumbs.
3. Whisk cream, egg and vanilla together in separate bowl.  Drizzle over flour mixture, add chocolate chips, and mix until everything appears moistened.
4. Work dough into ball and press in 8-inch disk on floured surface.
5. Cut into 8 wedges.
6. Place into tupperware and refrigerate over night. (optional)
7. Bake at 400 for 16 - 20 minutes.
