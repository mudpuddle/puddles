Ingredients

* * *

|     |        |                                                              |
| --- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | quart  | chicken broth                                                |
| 2½  | lbs    | potatoes, unpeeled, coarsely chopped                         |
| 2   | cup    | chopped green onions, whites and about half the green stalks |
| 1   | quart  | light cream                                                  |
| ¼   | cup    | soy sauce (or Kikkoman’s Ponzu sauce or Tempura sauce)       |
| 1   | tsp    | freshly ground white pepper                                  |
| 6   | ounces | shredded Swiss cheese                                        |
| 6   | ounces | shredded Cheddar cheese                                      |
| ½   | cup    | good-quality craft beer                                      |

Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. In a heavy-bottomed soup pot bring the chicken broth to a boil.
2. Add the potatoes and simmer for 30 minutes, or until the potatoes are very soft.
3. Add the green onions and remove the pot from the heat.
4. Add the light cream to the pot.
5. Puree the potato-broth mixture in an immersion blender, or in your blender or food processor (you will have to do this in batches; when blending, fill the container only half full and cover the lid with a dish towel because the soup "spurts" quite violently as it's being blended).
6. Return the puree to the pot.
7. Stir in the soy sauce and pepper and slowly bring the soup back to a simmer.
8. When ready to serve or pack into a thermos, proceed with the recipe by placing the pot back on the burner, over medium heat.
9. When the soup begins to simmer, stir in the grated cheeses gradually, a hand-full at a time.
10. Now gently whisk in the beer.

**NOTE:** The soup can be prepared through step 7 up to 48 hours ahead and refrigerated, or prepared and frozen for 3 months.

_Makes 8 servings_
