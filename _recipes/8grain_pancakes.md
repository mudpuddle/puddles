Ingredients

* * *

|     |      |                   |
| --- | ---- | ----------------- |
| ¼   | cup  | spelt flour       |
| ¼   | cup  | whole wheat flour |
| ¼   | cup  | oat flour         |
| ¼   | cup  | millet flour      |
| ¼   | cup  | rye flour         |
| ¼   | cup  | barley flour      |
| ¼   | cup  | corn meal         |
| ¼   | cup  | ground flax seed  |
| 2   | tsp  | baking powder     |
| ½   | tsp  | sea salt          |
| 2   | cups | non-dairy milk    |
| 3   | tbsp | olive oil         |
| 2   | tbsp | maple syrup       |
| 1   | tsp  | vanilla extract   |
| 1½  | cups | strawberries      |
| 1   | tsp  | coconut oil       |

Cooking Instructions

* * *

Combine the flours, ground seed, baking powder, and salt in a mixing bowl.  Add the mil, olive oil, sweetener, and vanilla and mix thoroughly.  fold in the strawberries.  Grease a skillet with the coconut oil and heat over medium-low heat for 3 to 5 minutes, or until a drop of water sizzles when it hits the pan.  Pour 1/2 to 3/4 cup of the batter onto the skillet for each pancake.  Cook until the bottom is golden brown and bubbles appear on top of the pancake, then flip to cook the other side. Repeat with the remaining batter.  Serve topped with maple syrup or your favorite fruit sauce.

_makes 10-12 6-inch pancakes_
