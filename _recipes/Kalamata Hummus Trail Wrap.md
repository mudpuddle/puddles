Ingredients

* * *

3     cups     cooked garbanzo beans
3     tbsp      tahini
3     tbsp      tamari, or 2 tbsp miso, or 2 tsp sea salt
1/4  cup       freshly squeezed lemon or lime juice
1/2  clove    garlic, chopped (optional)  
1     tsp        ground cumin
                    black pepper to taste
1/8  tsp        cayenne pepper (optional)  
                    chopped kalamata olives

Cooking Instructions

* * *

Place the beans, tahini, tamari, lemon juice, garlic, and cumin in a food processor or blender.  Process until smooth.  Add a small amount of water to keep the mixture moving if needed.  Season to taste with black and cayenne pepper.

For each wrap, spread a thin layer of hummus on a tortilla and sprinkle some of the olives in line down the center of the tortilla.  Roll the tortilla into tight wrap and cut into two or three pieces, depending on the size of the tortillas.

Pack the rolls in a small plastic bag and refrigerate overnight so the are ready for the next morning’s long run.  For a more substantial lunch, add lettuce, red bell pepper, and tomato before rolling the wraps.  Hummus keeps refrigerated for 5 to 6 days or freezes well for several months.

_makes 8-10 servings_
