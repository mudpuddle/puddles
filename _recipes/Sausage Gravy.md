Ingredients

* * *

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ½   | lbs | pork sausage |
| ¼   | cup | flour |
| 2   | cups | milk |
| ¼   | tsp | salt (to taste) |
| ¼   | tsp | black pepper (to taste) |

Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. Cook and crumble sausage until it is completely cooked.  DO NOT drain the grease.
2. Spread sausage out into even layer on bottom of pan.
3. Sprinkle flour over sausage and then stir well to combine.  Cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring often.
4. Whisk the milk into sausage and flour mixture.  Continue whisking until mixture starts to simmer.
5. Keep stirring, at low simmer, until graven begins to thicken.  1 - 3 minutes.
6. Serve over biscuits.
