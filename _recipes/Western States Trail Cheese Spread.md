Ingredients

* * *

1/2  16 oz    package firm tofu, drained
3     tbsp      while or yellow miso

3     tbsp      lemon juice
1/4  cup       tahini
2     tbsp      olive oil
1/4  cup       nutritional yeast 
3     tsp        paprika
1     tbsp      water
1/2  tsp        garlic powder 
1     tsp        onion powder 
1     tsp        dijon mustard

Cooking Instructions

* * *

Place all the ingredients in a blender of food processor and process for 2 to 3 minutes, until a smooth consistency is reached.  Spread a layer on whole grain bread (Ezekiel 4:9 sprouted) with sliced tomato and lettuce for a ‘cheese’ sandwich, or serve with crackers or raw veggies to dip.  Keeps about a week in the refrigerator or freeze for up to 2 months.

_makes 3 cups, 10-12 servings_
