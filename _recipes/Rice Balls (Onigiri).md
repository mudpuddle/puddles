Ingredients

* * *

2     cups     sushi rice
4     cups     water
2     tsp        miso
3-4  sheets  nori seaweed

Cooking Instructions

* * *

Cook the rice in the water and cool.

Fill small bowl with water and wet both hands so the rice does not stick.  Using your hands, form 1/4 cup rice into a triangle.  Spread 1/4 tsp miso evenly on one side of the triangle.  Cover with another 1/4 cup rice.  Shape into one triangle, making sure the miso is covered with rice.  Fold the nori sheets in half and then treat the apart.  Using half of one sheet, wrap the rice triangle in nori, making sure to completely cover the rice.  Repeat using the remaining rice, miso and nori.

_makes 8 onigiri_
