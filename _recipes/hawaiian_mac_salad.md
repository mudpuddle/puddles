Ingredients

* * *

|     |         |                                     |
| --- | ------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 1   | lbs     | elbow macaroni                      |
| 1   | package | frozen mixed veggies                |
| 1   | cup     | mayonnaise                          |
| ½   | tsp     | kosher salt                         |
| 2   | tbsp    | grated white onion                  |
| ¼   | tsp     | ground black pepper - more to taste |

Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. Cook macaroni until just tender in salted water. Drain and set aside in a large bowl to cool.
2. Add frozen mixed veggies while noodles are hot.
3. Once cooled, stir mayonnaise, salt, onion, and pepper into the noodles until well combined.
4. Store in the refrigerator until ready to serve. If the salad seems dry add a bit of milk, 1 tablespoon at a time or a little more mayonnaise.

_makes 12 servings_
