Ingredients

* * *

|     |        |                                                                                               |
| --- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 7½  | cups   | chicken broth                                                                                 |
| 2   | cups   | uncooked green lentils, washed and picked through for stones (1 pound)                        |
| 2   | tsp    | vegetable oil                                                                                 |
| 2   | lbs    | Creole sausage, linguica or chorizo, chopped into small pieces                                |
| 2   |        | onions, diced                                                                                 |
| 8   | cloves | garlic, peeled and minced                                                                     |
| ½   | cup    | dry white wine                                                                                |
| 20  | ounces | frozen chopped spinach (two 10-ounce packages) or 8 cups tightly packed chopped fresh spinach |
| 1   | can    | diced or crushed tomatoes with juice (28 ounces)                                              |
| ¼   | tsp    | ground white pepper                                                                           |
|     |        | chopped fresh parsley for garnish                                                             |

Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. In a soup pot, bring the broth to a boil, add the lentils, cover and simmer for about 45 minutes, or until the lentils are tender, but not falling apart. Stir the pot periodically to prevent sticking and burning.
2. While the lentils are cooking, heat a frying pan, add the vegetable oil, and saute the sausage until crisp and brown.
3. Push the sausage to one side, add the onions and garlic, and continue cooking until the onions become translucent. Remove from the heat.
4. When the lentils are tender, add the sausage mixture with the oil.
5. Stir in the dry white wine, the spinach, tomatoes, and pepper.
6. Bring the soup to a simmer and cook for an additional 15 minutes to merge the flavors.
7. Garnish each serving with chopped parsley.

_Makes 8 servings_
