Ingredients

* * *

|     |      |                 |
| --- | ---- | --------------- |
| 2   | cups | flour           |
| 4   | tsp  | baking powder   |
| 2   | tbsp | sugar           |
| ½   | tsp  | salt            |
| ½   | tsp  | cream of tartar |
| ½   | cup  | butter          |
| ¾   | cup  | milk            |

Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, salt, and cream of tartar.
3. Use Cheese grater to grate butter into bowl.  Stir until the butter is broken up and the mixture is well combined.
4. Add milk and stir until just combined - do not overmix.
5. Pat dough on a floured surface until ¾ inch thick.  Cut into biscuits.
6. Place on ungreased cookie sheet, being sure biscuits are touching.
7. Bake for 8-10 minutes, until golden brown.
