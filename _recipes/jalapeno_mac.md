Ingredients

* * *

|     |        |                                |
| --- | ------ | ------------------------------ |
| 6   | tbsp   | butter                         |
| 4   | slices | bread (crusts removed)         |
| 10  | strips | bacon                          |
| 2   |        | jalapenos                      |
| 4   | tbsp   | flour                          |
| 4   | cups   | milk                           |
| 2   | cups   | irish white cheddar (shredded) |
| 2½  | cups   | sharp cheddar (shredded)       |
| 1   | lbs    | pasta (cooked 2 mins less)     |


Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. Preheat oven to 350 and grease a medium baking dish
2. Melt 2 tbsp of the butter in a small bowl, toss it with the break pieces then set aside
3. Cook the bacon in a heavy bottomed pot.  When cooked remove it to a paper towel lined plate to drain
4. Remove most of the bacon grease from the pot, but leave any bits that are stuck to the bottom
5. Add the 4tbsp butter and jalapeño and cook over medium heat for about 2 mins, scraping up the bacon bits
6. Whisk in the flour, and cook - whisking constantly - for about 3 mins
7. Slowly whisk in the milk and cook, stirring constantly, until thickened and just about to simmer - about 5 mins, then remove from the heat
8. Generously add salt and pepper and stir in the white cheddar and two cups of the sharp cheddar - stir until melted, then fold in the bacon, jalapeños, and pasta
9. Pour the pasta into the prepared dish, toss the bread pieces with the remaining 1/2 cup of sharp cheddar and spread over the top
10. Bake for about 25 - 30 mins, until bubbly and the breadcrumbs are golden brown
