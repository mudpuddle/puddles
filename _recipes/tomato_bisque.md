Ingredients

* * *

|     |        |                                             |
| --- | ------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| 3   | tbsp   | butter                                      |
| 1½  | cups   | chopped yellow onion                        |
| 3   | cloves | garlic, minced                              |
| 1   | can    | Italian plum tomatoes, undrained (28 ounce) |
| 1   | tbsp   | chopped fresh basil (or 1 teaspoon dried)   |
| ½   | tsp    | dry mustard                                 |
| ¼-½ | tsp    | salt                                        |
| ¼   | tsp    | white pepper                                |
| ¼   | cup    | tomato paste                                |
| ¼   | cup    | flour                                       |
| 2   | cups   | chicken broth                               |
| 1   | cup    | half-and-half                               |
| 2-4 | tbsp   | dry sherry (optional)                       |

Garnish: For a wonderful flavor and texture contrast, I like to break up rounds of garlic flavored crostini into ½-inch pieces and pass around for diners to sprinkle on their soup

Cooking Instructions

* * *

1. In a deep saucepan, melt the butter and saute the onion and garlic until softened and lightly golden.
2. Add the tomatoes, basil, mustard, salt, pepper and tomato paste. Simmer, uncovered, for 10 minutes.
3. Whisk together the flour and about ½ cup of the broth to form a smooth paste, then stir this into the soup, along with the remaining 1½ cups of broth.
4. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer, stirring constantly, until the soup has thickened.
5. Remove from heat and puree in a food processor or blender in two or more batches (do not fill the container more than half full).
6. Return the puree to the pot and stir in the cream.
7. When ready to serve, gently reheat it, adjust the seasonings, then add the sherry to taste, if desired.

The soup can be prepared through step 6 and either refrigerated or frozen.

_Makes 6 servings_
