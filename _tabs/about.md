Welcome to my digital workbench and reference shelf.

This site is a simple, no-fuss repository for things I reach for regularly—a central place to stash notes, reference guides, and bookmarks so they don't get lost across browser tabs, notebooks, and scattered devices.

### What You'll Find Here

* **Tech Notes & Links:** Setup guides, code snippets, cheat sheets, and useful web tools that save time when building or troubleshooting.
* **Recipes:** Tried-and-true meals, go-to kitchen ratios, and cooking notes refined through repetition.
* **Fishing Info & Resources:** Local reports, knot guides, gear checklists, and links to current conditions before heading out on the water.

### Why This Exists

It's built simply using Jekyll to keep it fast, lightweight, and accessible from anywhere—whether I'm at my desk, standing in the kitchen, or checking conditions at the trailhead.

Feel free to look around; if you find something useful, consider it an open secret.
