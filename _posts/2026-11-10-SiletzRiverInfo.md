## River Access
1. Hee Hee Illahee Park Boat Ramp
* Public park in Siletz
* [google map](https://www.google.com/maps/@44.71951,-123.9157361,18.5z)
2. Old Mill Park
* Public park in Siletz
* [google map](https://www.google.com/maps/@44.7253028,-123.9166673,17.24z)
3. Ojalla Park
* Public boat slide just outside of Siletz
* Very steep, need a very long rope and leather gloves to use effectively.
* [google map](https://www.google.com/maps/@44.765074,-123.9143573,18z)
4. Jack Morgan Park
* Public park outside Siletz
* This boat ramp comes up very quickly and is covered by trees.  Don't miss it.
* [google map](https://www.google.com/maps/@44.8006762,-123.9041853,17z)

## Floats and Times
The Town Float
: from Illahee to Old Mill
: Can be done with out a shuttle, walking across town from from Old Mill back to Illahee
: ~2.5 hours

Ojalla to Morgan
: Nice float for Salmon
: ~4+ hours

## Shuttle Service
The Original Siletz River Shuttle Service
: link: <https://www.facebook.com/siletzrivershuttleservice/>
: phone: 541-270-1187
: cost: $25 - $30
: Stop at [Larry's Old Place](https://www.google.com/maps/@44.7230277,-123.9189406,18z) in Siletz grab an envelope from the box and fill it out.  Then drop it through the slot in the door.  If you are starting after 11:00, you will need to call to confirm availability.
